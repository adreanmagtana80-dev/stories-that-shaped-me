01 / The person behind the screen

Why stories matter to me

Stories can entertain us, but the ones we remember can also challenge our thinking and help us understand ourselves.

🎬

Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

2018 · Animated superhero film

The story follows Miles Morales, a teenager who becomes Spider-Man and meets other Spider-people from different dimensions.

Why I chose this movie

I like this movie because Miles is not someone who knows everything from the beginning. He feels uncertain, makes mistakes, and struggles to understand his new responsibilities. As the story continues, he learns to trust himself and discovers what kind of hero he wants to be.

The animation, music, action, and humor make the movie exciting, but its message about believing in yourself is what makes it memorable to me.

02 / Three stories, three perspectives

The stories I carry with me

These three stories stay with me for different reasons. Each one explores identity, courage, difficult choices, and the importance of holding on to what matters.

🕸️
Story 01 · Completed

Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

Miles' journey shows me that I do not need to be perfect before I begin. Confidence can grow as I take chances, learn, and keep going.

🔥
Story 02 · Courage & standing up

The Hunger Games

Katniss Everdeen's courage reminds me that standing up for what is right can matter, even when the situation feels unfair or intimidating. Her willingness to protect the people she cares about shows me that bravery is not only about being strong; it is also about compassion and making difficult choices.

🧟
Story 03 · Trust & humanity

The Walking Dead

Even in a world filled with uncertainty, the story shows how much people depend on trust, cooperation, and community. It reminds me that difficult circumstances can test people's values, and that protecting others and making thoughtful decisions are important.

03 / What stays with me

Lessons beyond the screen

These are the ideas I take from all three stories and want to remember in everyday life.

01

Believe in myself

I may feel unprepared sometimes. The movie reminds me that confidence can grow when I take the first step and keep learning.

02

Learn from mistakes

Mistakes do not mean I have failed forever. They can help me understand what to improve and make better choices next time.

03

Accept responsibility

My choices affect other people. I should be responsible for my actions and try to do what is right, even when it is difficult.

04

Be myself

I do not have to copy someone else to succeed. I can learn from others while developing my own strengths and identity.

05

Stand up for what is right — The Hunger Games

Katniss volunteering to take her sister's place shows me that courage can come from love and concern for others. It makes me think about speaking up when something is unfair.

06

Value trust and teamwork — The Walking Dead

The group's changing relationships remind me that people need one another. Trust, cooperation, and empathy can help a community face difficult situations.

05 / Personal reflection

How this movie affects my thinking and life

Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse affects the way I think because it reminds me that everyone has their own journey. Sometimes I compare myself to other people and feel that I am not good enough or that I am moving too slowly. Miles' journey teaches me that I do not need to be perfect or have everything figured out before I begin.

It also encourages me to face challenges instead of avoiding them. In school and in everyday life, I can make mistakes, learn from them, and continue improving. The movie reminds me to take responsibility, believe in my abilities, and stay true to who I am.

My biggest takeaway: I do not have to be someone else to make a difference. I just have to believe in myself and keep trying.

What I take from The Hunger Games: Katniss reminds me that one person's decision to protect someone or challenge unfairness can have a wider meaning. I want to remember that doing the right thing can require courage, especially when it is not the easiest choice.

What I take from The Walking Dead: The series makes me think about how important trust and community are. People may have different opinions, but listening, helping one another, and thinking carefully about our choices can make a difference.
